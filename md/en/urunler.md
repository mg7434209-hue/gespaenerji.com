## About the packages

### What's included in the package prices?

Packages include A-brand panels, inverter, mounting structure, DC/AC cabling, switchboard and turnkey workmanship. Shown figures are estimates; the exact price is set during a roof/land survey.

### Are the shown prices final?

No. Prices are approximate starting values; they vary with roof type, orientation/tilt, distance and equipment choice. A firm quote is prepared after a free survey.

### Can I scale the package to my needs?

Yes. All packages scale to your consumption profile and can be customized with a battery, extra panels or a different inverter.

Use our free calculator based on your bill or consumption; see the suitable system size and pick the closest package.
