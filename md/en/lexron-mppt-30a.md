Lexron 30 A MPPT has a 30 A charging current for 12/24 V battery systems. The model and battery voltages are printed on the label on the unit.

MPPT (maximum power point tracking) finds the highest power the panel can deliver in the current light and passes it to the battery. When the panel voltage is higher than the battery voltage, the difference is not wasted but turned into charging current. That is why an MPPT controller collects more energy from the same panel than a PWM controller.

Installation

## How is it connected?

The panel connects to the charge controller and the controller to the battery; the inverter and large loads are fed directly from the battery.

- ☀️ **Solar panel**DC voltage
- 📟 **Lexron 30 A MPPT**12/24 V
- 🔋 **Battery**12/24 V
- 🔌 **Inverter / DC load**straight to the battery
- 1 ### Connect the battery first General rule: the controller is connected to the battery first and the panel last. Put a suitable fuse on the battery cable.
- 2 ### Connect the panel Extend the panel cables with MC4 connectors; connect plus and minus to the controller's panel input the right way round.
- 3 ### Connect the loads Connect the inverter directly to the battery. If the controller has a load output, use it only for small DC loads such as lighting.

**Installation tips**

- Mount the controller close to the battery in a ventilated, dry place.
- Keep the battery cable short and thick; long thin cable causes voltage loss.
- If you will use a lithium battery, send us its charging values before ordering.

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently asked

### Should I choose PWM or MPPT?

If the panel voltage is well above the battery voltage (like the 285 W and 655 W panels in our shop), MPPT collects more energy from the same panel and is the right choice. For small, budget-first systems where the panel voltage is close to the battery voltage, PWM models are enough.

### How much panel power can I connect?

Rule of thumb: charging current × battery voltage. For 30 A that is about 360 W at 12 V, about 720 W at 24 V. This is an estimate, not a datasheet value; write to us for the maximum panel power and voltage the manufacturer allows.

### How many panels can I wire in series?

In series wiring the limit is set by the controller's maximum panel input voltage, which is not in the product information we have. Send us the Voc of the panel you will use and we'll check the layout together before you order.

### Can I use it with a lithium battery?

Battery-type settings are in the manufacturer's datasheet and are not in the product information we have. Send us your battery type and charging values and we'll confirm with the manufacturer before you order.
