# Solar Energy Storage and Battery Systems

A battery stores solar electricity for later use. Selection depends on daily consumption, critical loads, inverter compatibility and backup duration.

## kW versus kWh

kW describes simultaneous load power; kWh describes stored energy. Inverter power, appliance starting demand and usable capacity are checked together.

## Will it work during an outage?

Not every battery system automatically powers the whole building. Backup output, compatible inverter, protection and critical circuits must be designed. Compatibility is checked before retrofitting.

## Information needed for a quote

Share appliances, operating hours, existing inverter model and backup time. The System Builder provides an initial requirements list; final selection follows technical review.
