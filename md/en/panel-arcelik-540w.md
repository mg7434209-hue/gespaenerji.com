Installation

## How is it connected?

The panel connects to a charge controller (battery system) or to an inverter's panel input (grid-tied system).

- ☀️ **Solar panel**540 W
- 🔗 **MC4**solar cable
- 📟 **Charge controller / inverter**MPPT · PWM
- 🔋 **Battery / grid**by system
- 1 ### Choose the spot Choose a south-facing spot without shade all day; even partial shade noticeably cuts output.
- 2 ### Fix it in place Fix the panel to a suitable mounting structure through the holes in its frame; never put load on the glass or the junction box.
- 3 ### Connect it Connect to the solar cable with MC4 connectors; in series wiring, the sum of the panels' Voc must not exceed the device's panel input limit.

**Installation tips**

- Keep the panel free of dust and leaves; dirty glass lowers output.
- In areas that drop below −10 °C in winter, check the series layout with us; Voc rises in the cold.

## Frequently asked

### Can I get the datasheet?

This panel's detailed datasheet is not on the site. Write to us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm values such as Voc, Isc, dimensions and weight with the manufacturer.

### Can I connect the panel straight to a battery?

No. There must always be a charge controller between the panel and the battery; otherwise the battery gets overcharged. If the panel voltage is well above the battery voltage choose MPPT, if it is close choose PWM.

### How are large panels shipped?

Large panels are shipped by courier or freight in breakage-protected packaging. Check the package in front of the courier on delivery; for damaged goods all costs are ours. For wholesale shipment by the pallet, write to us.
