## Basic questions about solar power

### What is the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid systems?

An on-grid system works connected to the grid: the electricity it produces is used in the building first and the surplus is fed into the grid. An off-grid system is independent of the grid; it generates and stores its own electricity with panels, a battery and an inverter, and is used where there is no grid, such as cabins, caravans or fields. A hybrid system combines the two: it is grid-connected but also uses a battery and keeps selected loads running during an outage.

### Does a solar system keep working during a power cut?

Grid-connected systems without a battery shut down automatically during an outage to protect crews working on the grid. If you want power during outages too, you need a hybrid or off-grid system with a battery; which appliances are backed up is defined in the design.

### Do panels produce electricity on cloudy days and in winter?

Yes. Panels generate from diffuse light as well as direct sunshine; output drops on cloudy days and short winter days but does not stop. Savings estimates are based on annual yield, so seasonal differences are already taken into account.

### How many years do solar panels last?

Quality panels generate electricity for decades; manufacturers usually give a 25–30-year power (performance) warranty. Panel efficiency declines slowly over the years; the annual loss rate and the warranty period are stated in each panel's manufacturer data sheet.

### Are the panels resistant to hail and storms?

Certified panels pass the hail and mechanical load tests of the IEC 61215 standard. Wind and snow load ratings are stated in each panel's data sheet, and the mounting system must also be chosen for the local wind conditions.

### Can I install a product I bought online myself?

Plug-and-play kits need no technical knowledge: place the panel in a shade-free spot and connect the colour-coded cables. Connections to the grid or to an electrical panel must be made by a qualified electrician; in the Antalya region we offer optional on-site installation.

## Rooftop solar installation

### Can panels be installed on tile, sandwich-panel and concrete roofs?

Yes. The mounting system is chosen for the roof type: hooks on tile roofs, rails and clamps on trapezoidal and sandwich panels, and tilted frames on flat concrete roofs. Whether the roof structure is suitable is checked during the site survey.

### Can panels go on a north-facing or shaded roof?

They can, but output is lower; south, east and west orientations are more productive. Because shade can also reduce the output of the whole string, a shading analysis is done during the site survey and the layout is adjusted if needed.

### How long does a rooftop installation take?

On-site installation depends on the system size; for homes it usually takes a few days. The overall timeline is mostly set by the distribution company's application and approval steps and becomes clear after the site survey.

### Will the weight of the panels harm the roof?

Panels and the mounting system add load to the roof. Whether the structure can carry this load together with wind and snow effects is assessed during the site survey; if needed, a structural review is requested.

## Ground-mounted solar

### How much land does a ground-mounted plant need?

The area needed depends on the target capacity, the row spacing and the slope of the land. The layout and row-spacing tools in the calculator give a first estimate; the final layout is set during the site survey and design.

### Can a solar plant be built on agricultural land?

The land's classification and permitted use are subject to the rules of the relevant authorities, so suitability is verified separately for each project. For irrigation systems, pump demand and generation are planned together.

### Why does row spacing matter?

In winter the sun is low, so a front row can shade the one behind it. Row spacing is calculated from the latitude and panel tilt to limit this shading; the row-spacing tool in the calculator gives a first value.

### Why is grid connection capacity checked at the start?

For the electricity to be fed into the grid, the connection point needs enough capacity. Capacity and connection conditions are confirmed through the distribution company's process, so the installed capacity is planned around them.

## Energy storage and batteries

### Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

It depends on whether the existing inverter supports a battery. With a hybrid inverter the battery can be added directly; otherwise a separate AC-coupled battery inverter is used. Compatibility is confirmed by a technical review.

### How is battery capacity calculated?

Daily consumption (kWh) is multiplied by the desired days of autonomy and then divided by the battery's usable depth of discharge and the system efficiency. The battery sizing tool in the calculator does this with your own figures.

### How is battery life expressed?

Battery life is given as a number of cycles (charge–discharge rounds) rather than years and depends on the chemistry, depth of discharge and temperature. Each product's cycle life is stated in the manufacturer's data sheet.

### Where should the battery be installed?

In a ventilated place out of direct sunlight, protected from moisture and within the temperature range stated by the manufacturer. High temperatures shorten battery life.

## Maintenance and monitoring

### How often should a solar plant be maintained?

The frequency is planned according to dust build-up, local conditions and the size of the plant. When monitoring data shows an unexpected drop in output, an extra inspection is carried out.

### How should the panels be cleaned?

Following the manufacturer's instructions, with non-abrasive materials and suitable water; high-pressure water and hard brushes can damage the surface. Working on a roof requires safety measures.

### What does remote monitoring provide?

Current and past output are tracked through the inverter and the monitoring platform, so faults and warnings are spotted early and production losses do not go unnoticed for long.

### What happens if the plant is not maintained?

Soiling, loosened connections and unnoticed inverter faults reduce output and in some cases create a safety risk. Regular inspection and monitoring catch these losses early.

## Building an off-grid system

### How many panels and batteries does my cottage need?

Appliance power and daily runtime determine the requirement. Enter device quantities and hours in the System Builder to estimate daily kWh, panel kWp, battery kWh and inverter kW.

### How is off-grid inverter power selected?

The inverter must cover simultaneous loads and motor starting demand. The System Builder includes simultaneity and startup allowance to suggest minimum power before you select a hybrid or off-grid model.

### Should I choose LiFePO₄ or gel batteries?

LiFePO₄ offers deeper discharge (about 90%) and longer cycle life, with higher upfront cost but lower lifetime cost per kWh. Gel batteries cost less initially but have shorter life. Compare both in the System Builder.

## PV solar water heater

### Is there hot water when there's no sun?

Yes. On cloudy/rainy days or at night, the system automatically switches to grid (AC) heating based on water temperature, ensuring hot water year-round.

### Which capacity should I choose?

Models from 60–200 L are available by household size. 60–80 L for 1–2 people, 100–120 L for 3–4, 150–200 L for 5+.

### What is the difference between horizontal and vertical models?

Horizontal models (60/80/100 L) suit compact spaces and balconies; vertical models (120/150/200 L) are ideal for large families and high consumption.

### Does it save on the electricity bill?

On sunny days heating is largely free, saving significantly versus a traditional electric boiler. The exact figure depends on usage and region.

### What about installation and warranty?

Roof/terrace integrated installation is done by our team. Enamel inner tank and A-brand equipment ensure long life; details are confirmed during a free survey.

### How is it different from an evacuated-tube collector system?

In evacuated-tube systems the water circulates inside the tubes, bringing risks of scaling, freezing and tube breakage. In a photovoltaic system the panel only generates electricity and water never enters it — the plumbing is simple, there is no freezing risk in winter, and the panel can go on the roof while the tank stays indoors.

### Does it work in winter and in frost?

Yes. The water is inside the insulated tank, not in the panel, and the control unit has freeze protection. On days when the sun isn't enough, grid support kicks in.

### Can it replace my existing electric boiler?

Usually yes. Connection dimensions are standard and the existing hot/cold water lines can be used. The mounting surface must carry the filled weight and a shade-free spot is needed for the panel — we check both during the survey.

### Is maintenance difficult, what do I have to do?

There are no moving parts. Dusting the panel once or twice a year, checking the relief valve annually and inspecting the magnesium anode every 2–4 years depending on water hardness is enough.

## EV solar conversion (BOOST MPPT)

### Will a solar panel fully charge my electric vehicle?

The contribution depends on panel power and daily sunshine. A panel that fits on a vehicle roof supports the pack through the day rather than filling it from empty on its own: it reduces charging drawn from the grid and keeps the battery topped up while parked. We calculate the realistic contribution for your battery capacity together during the survey.

### My vehicle's battery is not lithium. Can it still be installed?

Yes. The unit works with AGM (VRLA), gel (VRLA), flooded lead-acid and lithium batteries; a user profile lets charge parameters match the battery maker's recommended values. We configure the correct profile during installation, so we confirm the battery type at the site assessment.

### My vehicle's battery voltage is different — is it compatible?

The unit supports 24, 36, 48, 60 and 72 V battery systems, with a panel input range of 15–60 V DC. Systems outside that range need a different solution — share your vehicle's rating plate and we will pick the right hardware together.

### Does it also charge while the vehicle is moving?

The panel generates whenever it receives light, so the system works both parked and moving. Output is highest during the hours the vehicle stands unshaded in the open.

## 72 V LiFePO₄ traction battery

### My vehicle has a gel battery — can I swap it for this?

Yes — on 72 V systems it goes in where the gel or lead-acid pack was. The charger must be LiFePO₄-compatible (CC/CV, 87.6 V); an old gel charger will not charge this pack correctly.

### Will the battery fit my vehicle's battery compartment?

The pack measures about 41 × 16 × 17 cm. Before ordering, compare this with the inside dimensions of your vehicle's battery compartment. If you are unsure, send us the compartment measurements via WhatsApp and we will check them together.

### How far does one charge take me?

Range depends on the vehicle's motor power, load, tyre pressure and road gradient, so we do not quote a figure in kilometres. The one certain number is the energy: 76.8 V × 30 Ah ≈ 2,300 Wh. If you know your vehicle's consumption we can work it out together.

### Is a charger included?

No — the battery is sold on its own. A suitable charger is a LiFePO₄-compatible CC/CV type: charge voltage 87.6 V, recommended charge current 6–10 A, and the highest charge current the battery accepts is 30 A. We can check your existing charger together before you order.

### How long does charging take?

With a 10 A fast charger it is fully charged in 3–4 hours. Charge at an ambient temperature between 0 °C and 45 °C; a LiFePO₄ battery must not be charged below 0 °C.

### Does charging it before it is empty harm the battery?

No. LiFePO₄ batteries have no memory effect; charging before the battery is empty, or unplugging before it is full, does not shorten its life.

### How many years will it last?

With proper use it is rated for 2,000 cycles. Depending on how often you use it, that means up to 10 years of service — many times the typical life of a gel battery.

### Is a LiFePO₄ battery safe? Is there a fire risk?

LiFePO₄ is far more thermally stable than other lithium-ion chemistries such as NMC; the risk of fire and explosion is markedly lower. The built-in BMS protects against overcharge, over-discharge and short circuit. Even so, use only a LiFePO₄-compatible charger, and do not puncture or crush the battery or bring it near an open flame.

### Can I charge it from a solar panel?

Yes. For charging a 72 V pack straight from a panel we use the BOOST MPPT 24–72 V charge controller; it is also in our ready-made e-trike solar sets.

## Pool safety — AI Lifeguard

### Does the system replace the lifeguard?

No — and it shouldn't. The international ISO 20380 standard defines these systems as a secondary supervision layer supporting the lifeguard. It extends your lifeguard's field of view and reaction speed.

### Can we reduce our lifeguard count once the system is installed?

No. Lifeguard numbers and qualifications are set by regulation; the system does not reduce or remove that obligation. ISO 20380 likewise defines this technology as a secondary surveillance layer supporting the lifeguard. Its contribution is not staff savings: it extends your existing team's field of view to the whole pool, delivers location information within seconds at the moment of risk, and turns every alert into a record you can show in audits.

### Can it be installed in our existing pool? How long does installation take?

Yes. In most projects installation is possible without draining the pool or closing the business; cabling and mounting can be scheduled for hours when the pool is closed. Total duration depends on the number of pools and existing infrastructure; the exact schedule is committed in the written quote after the free survey. After installation the system is calibrated for your facility and your lifeguard team receives on-site training. Facilities that prefer not to start mid-season can reserve next season's installation slot now.

### Does it work with our existing CCTV cameras?

Standard security cameras are designed for general surveillance; reflections, glare and viewing-angle issues on the water surface usually make them insufficient for drowning detection. The system runs on its own cameras designed for this task. That said, your existing cabling, network and recording infrastructure is assessed during the free survey; suitable parts can be reused, which can reduce installation time and cost.

### How many cameras are needed?

The camera count is engineered from the pool's surface area, shape, depth zones and blind spots; the goal is continuous coverage of the entire surface. A single regular-shaped pool may need only a few cameras, while pools with slides or divided sections need a larger coverage plan. The exact count and layout are presented in the written quote with a coverage sketch prepared at the free survey.

### Are there false alarms?

No system can promise zero false alarms; quality systems are designed to keep them to a few per day and are calibrated per facility. Every alert is stored as a record and report.

### How is guest privacy protected?

Footage is processed on an in-facility server and is not transferred to the cloud or abroad. The recording policy is configured to keep only incident moments, in line with data-protection law, and privacy notices are displayed at facility entrances.

### Do we have any obligations for KVKK (data protection) compliance?

With installation you receive pool-specific privacy notice and retention policy templates; your facility's main step is displaying these visibly at entrances and adding them to your existing KVKK documentation. Cameras are engineered to see only the pool and its surroundings; changing cabins, showers and similar private areas are excluded from coverage. Footage is processed on the on-site local server and is not transferred to the cloud or abroad. For the final legal assessment we recommend having your own KVKK counsel approve the texts.
