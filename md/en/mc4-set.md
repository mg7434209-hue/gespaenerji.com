Installation

## How is it connected?

The panel cable joins the solar cable via MC4, and the solar cable connects to the charge controller.

- ☀️ **Solar panel**panel cable
- 🔗 **MC4**male + female
- 〰️ **Solar cable**black − · red +
- 📟 **Charge controller**panel input
- 1 ### Measure Measure the distance between panel and device; do not leave the cable needlessly long.
- 2 ### Fit the connector Crimp the MC4 connector onto the cable end; use red for the plus line and black for the minus line.
- 3 ### Connect it Connect the device to the battery first, then the panel to the device; check the polarity.

**Installation tips**

- Route the cable away from sharp edges and hot surfaces.
