### How are wholesale prices set?

Wholesale prices are tiered by product and order quantity. Send your list via WhatsApp or the form; a proforma quote is sent the same day.

Listed items are physical stock. After order and payment confirmation dispatch is scheduled quickly; delivery by contracted carrier to every province of Türkiye.

We sell wholesale with corporate invoicing to dealers, EPC and installation companies, electrical wholesalers, hotel/site managements and agricultural cooperatives.

Bank transfer against a proforma invoice. Dispatch follows payment confirmation.

We work with tax-registered businesses in electrical, solar, caravan-camping or hardware retail. Regular dealers receive marketing assets, product training and special terms; details are agreed in a meeting.
