Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently asked

### How many years does a solar plant take to pay back?

Typically 3–6 years depending on consumption profile, region and electricity price. Get an estimate with our calculator.

### How do I know if my roof is suitable?

Roof orientation, tilt, shading and load-bearing structure matter. Our field team assesses suitability during the free survey.

### How long is the panel warranty?

Our A-brand panels carry up to 25-year performance warranty; inverters 5–12 years product warranty.

### Can I sell my surplus energy?

In unlicensed generation, surplus energy is handled under net metering. We share the details based on your project.

### Do you provide financing support?

Yes. We advise on loan/leasing and incentive applications with partner institutions.
