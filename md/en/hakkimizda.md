Our Corporate History

## Turkey's pioneering solar energy implementers

GESPA started in the early days when very few companies in Turkey applied solar energy. For over two decades, we source products directly from manufacturers, transform field-learned insights into products, and build systems with our own engineering team.

🏁 **First Generation in the Sector**Continuous field experience since 2005, from the very beginning of photovoltaic applications in Turkey.

🌍 **Import and Export**Direct imports from manufacturers for panels, inverters and batteries; system and product exports for international projects.

🔬 **Research and Product Leadership**From solar water heaters to AI-powered safety systems; pioneering implementations of new product categories entering the market.

- 2005 Founding **Starting Among the Sector's First Wave**We started operations in Manavgat when very few companies were implementing solar energy in Turkey; our first photovoltaic system sales and commissioning projects were completed during this era.
- 2008 Supply Chain **Direct Import from Manufacturers**We established direct relationships with manufacturers for panels, inverters and batteries; the direct supply chain provided permanent advantages in pricing and delivery time. During the same period, we completed our 100th off-grid system installation.
- 2012 Research **Leadership in New Product Categories**We brought new product categories entering the Turkish market—such as solar water heaters and solar-powered irrigation pumps—to the field; we launched product development work based on usage data.
- 2015 Scaling **Transition to Industrial Scale**We transitioned from residential applications to industrial and tourism facilities; end-to-end engineering–supply–construction (EPC) competence in industrial rooftop and land-based projects, including distribution company approval processes, became part of our service.
- 2019 Export **Cross-Border Supply**We began exporting—supplying panels, inverters and complete systems for international projects; our global manufacturer and supplier network expanded.
- 2022 Incorporation **Gespa Enerji Sanayi Ticaret Ltd. Şti.**With growing project volume and corporate partnerships, we restructured our operations under a limited company umbrella; our trade registry registration was completed on 01.11.2022.
- 2023 Growth **15 MW Installed Capacity**We reached over 15 MW of installed capacity in the region; remote monitoring and maintenance (O&M) services became a standard part of every project.
- 2026 Innovation **500+ Projects and Expanding Product Family**Over 500 completed projects across Antalya; we expanded our product family with GES Marketim e-store, B2B wholesale supply and the AI-powered AI Lifeguard Support System.

Our activities under the GESPA brand have continued uninterrupted in Manavgat since 2005; corporate restructuring was completed on 01.11.2022 with registration of Gespa Enerji Sanayi Ticaret Limited Şirketi in the trade registry.
