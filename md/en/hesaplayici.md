Frequently Asked Questions

## About the calculation

### How is the cost of a solar plant calculated?

Installation cost is proportional to system size (kWp): the calculator uses the current estimated cost per kWp and adds storage cost if a battery is selected. The exact price is finalized after a free site survey.

### How accurate are the calculator results?

Results are estimates based on realistic assumptions including regional yield, roof orientation and panel degradation; exact values are determined by site survey and engineering.

### How many panels and how much roof area does 1 kWp require?

It depends on panel wattage: the calculator uses the default panel power and approximate roof area per kWp shown in the Assumptions box; panel count and required area appear automatically on the result card.

### How is the payback period calculated?

Savings from annual production are accumulated year by year with panel degradation and an electricity price scenario; the year cumulative savings exceed the investment is the payback period. In Türkiye it is typically 3–6 years.
