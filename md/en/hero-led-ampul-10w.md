Installation

## How is it connected?

In a battery system the bulb is fed from the battery or from the charge controller's load output.

- ☀️ **Solar panel**DC voltage
- 📟 **Charge controller**PWM · MPPT
- 🔋 **Battery**12 V / 24 V
- 💡 **Hero LED**10 W
- 1 ### Socket and cable Fit the bulb into a suitable socket; connect the socket cable to the battery through a fuse or to the charge controller's load output.
- 2 ### Check polarity On DC, connect plus and minus the right way round.
- 3 ### Use a switch Put a switch on the line; leaving the bulb on drains the battery.

**Installation tips**

- Check the voltage values when wiring and mounting; if in doubt, write to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently asked

### Can it be connected straight to a 12 V battery?

Yes. The bulb works on 12–60 V AC/DC; in 12 V and 24 V battery systems it runs straight from the battery without an inverter.

### Can it be used on mains power (230 V)?

No. Its operating range is 12–60 V; it must not be plugged into 230 V mains.

### How long does it run on a battery?

The bulb draws 10 W. Rough estimate: the usable energy of a 12 V 100 Ah battery (≈ 600 Wh at 50% discharge) runs one bulb for about 60 hours. The actual time depends on battery type and condition.
