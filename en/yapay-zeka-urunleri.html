How does it work?

It sees, understands and alerts

  1. 1

    Sees

    Your suitable IP cameras, or new cameras chosen for the job, monitor the area without interruption.

  2. 2

    Understands

    The image is analysed by AI in the camera itself or in a local recorder on site; the type of event and its location are identified.

  3. 3

    Alerts

    When an event is detected, an alert goes out within seconds by phone notification, audible and visual alarm, smartwatch or control panel, and the footage of the event is recorded.

Data and privacy

The solutions we recommend detect events, not identities: a person, a vehicle, smoke, a fall or a missing hard hat, for example. Facial recognition involves biometric data, which Turkish data protection law (KVKK) treats as a special category of personal data; it is considered separately and only in projects with a legal basis.

In areas monitored by cameras, the KVKK duty to inform applies: a warning sign and a privacy notice are required. We help you prepare them during installation.
