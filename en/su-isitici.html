How Does It Work?

Free hot water in 3 steps

The photovoltaic panel feeds electricity directly to the water heater — simple, safe and efficient.

  1. 1

    Photovoltaic Module

    The monocrystalline panel turns sunlight into electricity.

  2. 2

    Water Heater Tank

    The enamel inner tank keeps heat in with polyurethane insulation.

  3. 3

    Hot Water Use

    Bathroom, kitchen — uninterrupted hot water for the whole home.

☁️ What happens on cloudy / rainy days?

The system automatically switches to grid power — hot water all year round. The smart heating strategy uses free solar on sunny days and cheap off-peak electricity at night.
