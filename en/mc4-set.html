Installation

How is it connected?

The panel cable joins the solar cable via MC4, and the solar cable connects to the charge controller.

  1. ☀️Solar panelpanel cable
  2. 🔗MC4male + female
  3. 〰️Solar cableblack − · red +
  4. 📟Charge controllerpanel input
  1. 1

    Measure

    Measure the distance between panel and device; do not leave the cable needlessly long.

  2. 2

    Fit the connector

    Crimp the MC4 connector onto the cable end; use red for the plus line and black for the minus line.

  3. 3

    Connect it

    Connect the device to the battery first, then the panel to the device; check the polarity.

Installation tips
  • Route the cable away from sharp edges and hot surfaces.
