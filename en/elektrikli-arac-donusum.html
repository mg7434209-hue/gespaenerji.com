How Does It Work?

From panel to wheel in 4 steps

The chain is simple: the sun generates, the controller manages, the battery stores, the vehicle uses.

  1. 1

    Solar Panel

    Mounted on the vehicle roof or upper platform, the panel produces direct current throughout the day.

  2. 2

    BOOST MPPT Charger

    It steps the panel voltage up to the battery pack level and, with its MPPT algorithm, draws the maximum power available in the current light.

  3. 3

    Lithium Battery

    Charge current is delivered to the pack under control. Settings are programmed to the pack's own ratings.

  4. 4

    Electric Vehicle

    The vehicle keeps running off the same battery pack, and its need for grid charging drops.

Frequently Asked Questions

About electric vehicle conversion

The contribution depends on panel power and daily sunshine. A panel that fits on a vehicle roof supports the pack through the day rather than filling it from empty on its own: it reduces charging drawn from the grid and keeps the battery topped up while parked. We calculate the realistic contribution for your battery capacity together during the survey.

The BOOST MPPT Charger is positioned for lithium battery packs. For other battery chemistries we assess suitability project by project; since the wrong charge profile shortens battery life, we confirm the battery type before installation.

The device is compatible with 48 V, 60 V and 72 V systems. Other voltages need a different solution; share your vehicle's rating plate values and we will identify suitable hardware together.

Charge parameters can be programmed from a phone and system status monitored. We set everything to your battery pack during installation; afterwards you can follow generation and charge status yourself if you wish.

The panel generates whenever it receives light, so the system works both parked and moving. Output is highest during the hours the vehicle stands unshaded in the open.

Manufacturers' warranty terms differ from brand to brand. We review your vehicle's warranty status with you before installation and never proceed without clearing it with you first.
