How Does It Work?

From panel to wheel in 4 steps

The chain is simple: the sun generates, the controller manages, the battery stores, the vehicle uses.

  1. 1

    Solar Panel

    Mounted on the vehicle roof or upper platform, the panel produces direct current throughout the day.

  2. 2

    BOOST MPPT Charger

    It steps the panel voltage up to the battery pack level and, with its MPPT algorithm, draws the maximum power available in the current light.

  3. 3

    Lithium Battery

    Charge current is delivered to the pack under control. Settings are programmed to the pack's own ratings.

  4. 4

    Electric Vehicle

    The vehicle keeps running off the same battery pack, and its need for grid charging drops.

Battery Compatibility

Works with every battery type

The unit is not limited to lithium: AGM, gel and flooded lead-acid packs are supported too. With the user profile, charge parameters can be programmed to the values recommended by the battery manufacturer.

  • AGM (VRLA) — sızdırmaz yapı, bakım gerektirmez
  • GEL (VRLA) — derin deşarja dayanıklı, uzun ömürlü
  • Sulu kurşun-asit — yaygın ve ekonomik çözüm
  • Lityum (LiFePO₄) — farklı BMS'li paketlerle uyumlu çalışma
  • Derin döngü — yüksek çevrim ömrü, derin deşarj toleransı
  • Kullanıcı profili — parametreler elle programlanabilir

Bluetooth monitoring and programming

The new model has no external display; operating values and charge parameters are managed from your phone over Bluetooth. The connection need not stay on — the unit keeps running independently with its last saved settings.
