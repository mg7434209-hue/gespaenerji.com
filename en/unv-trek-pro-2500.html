The Trek Pro 2500 is a portable power station built to take mains power with you wherever you need a socket. Its 2,496 Wh battery runs devices drawing up to 2,500 W independently of the grid.

The outputs are pure sine wave, so waveform-sensitive equipment — refrigerators, boilers, computers and measuring instruments — runs without distortion. Alongside the four 230 V sockets on top there are two Type-C ports (200 W and 100 W), four USB-A ports and 12 V DC outputs — 14 devices can be powered at once.

If the power fails while the unit is connected to the mains, it switches to its own battery within 30 milliseconds. Your computer, modem or camera recorder stays on, and no separate UPS is needed.

Use it for camping and caravanning, on a site or in a garden with no mains supply, at temporary set-ups such as weddings and events, and as home backup power. Solar panel not included.
