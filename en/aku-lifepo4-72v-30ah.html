Most e-trikes ship with a gel or flooded lead-acid battery pack. Those types are cheap but heavy, lose capacity within a few hundred cycles, and their voltage sags past the halfway point, so the vehicle noticeably loses power.

LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry solves all three: it carries the same energy in a much lighter body, lasts 3,000–6,000 charge cycles per the manufacturer's data sheet, and holds its voltage steady while discharging — the motor does not lose power as the pack empties.

This 24S pack gives 30 Ah at 72.0 V nominal — about 2,160 Wh of energy. It carries a balancing BMS (battery management system) inside: it keeps the cells even and protects against overcharge, over-discharge and short circuit.

The charger is not included. A suitable one is a LiFePO₄-compatible CC/CV type: charge voltage about 83.95 V, recommended charge current 6–10 A. The old charger you used for a gel battery will not charge this pack correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked

My vehicle has a gel battery — can I swap it for this?

Yes — on 72 V systems it goes in where the gel or lead-acid pack was. The charger must be LiFePO₄-compatible (CC/CV, ~83.95 V); an old gel charger will not charge this pack correctly.

How far does one charge take me?

Range depends on the vehicle's motor power, load, tyre pressure and road gradient, so we do not quote a figure in kilometres. The one certain number is the energy: 72 V × 30 Ah ≈ 2,160 Wh. If you know your vehicle's consumption we can work it out together.

Is a charger included?

No — the battery is sold on its own. A suitable charger is a LiFePO₄-compatible CC/CV type: charge voltage ~83.95 V, recommended charge current 6–10 A. We can check your existing charger together before you order.

How many years will it last?

The manufacturer's data sheet gives 3,000 – 6,000 charge cycles. At one full cycle a day that is many times the typical life of a gel battery; the exact figure depends on how hard you use it.

Can I charge it from a solar panel?

Yes. For charging a 72 V pack straight from a panel we use the BOOST MPPT 24–72 V charge controller; it is also in our ready-made e-trike solar sets.

Is your vehicle not 72 V?

We also source batteries for 24, 36, 48 and 60 V systems — write to us for stock and pricing.
