Most e-trikes ship with a gel or flooded lead-acid battery pack. Those types are cheap but heavy, lose capacity within a few hundred cycles, and their voltage sags past the halfway point, so the vehicle noticeably loses power.

LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry solves all three: it carries the same energy in a body up to 3 times lighter than gel batteries, lasts 2,000 cycles with proper use, and holds its voltage steady while discharging — the motor does not lose power as the pack empties.

This 24S pack gives 30 Ah at 76.8 V nominal (24 cells × 3.2 V) — about 2,300 Wh of energy. It carries a balancing BMS (battery management system) inside: it keeps the cells even and protects against overcharge, over-discharge and short circuit.

The charger is not included. A suitable one is a LiFePO₄-compatible CC/CV type. The maximum charge voltage is 87.6 V: the safe charge limit of a LiFePO₄ cell is 3.65 V, and 24 cells × 3.65 V = 87.6 V. The recommended charge current is 6–10 A; the highest charge current the battery accepts is 30 A. Charge between 0 °C and 45 °C. The old charger you used for a gel battery will not charge this pack correctly.

Why LiFePO₄?

How does it differ from a gel battery?

It drops into the same 72 V system in place of the gel pack; the difference shows in lifespan, weight and how the vehicle feels to ride.

🔁

Long life

Rated for 2,000 cycles with proper use — up to 10 years of service, depending on how often you use it.

🪶

Lightweight design

Up to 3 times lighter than gel batteries and takes up less space — less dead weight to haul around.

⚡

Fast charging

A 10 A fast charger fills it completely in 3–4 hours.

📈

Full performance

You use the full capacity without losing power; the voltage stays steady throughout the discharge, so it performs strongly on hills.

🛡️

High level of safety

LiFePO₄ is far more thermally stable than other lithium-ion chemistries such as NMC; the risk of fire and explosion is markedly lower.

🔄

No memory effect

Charging before it is empty, or unplugging before it is full, does not shorten its life.

💰

Economical

Thanks to its long life it works out cheaper than gel batteries in the long run.
