Rooftop Solar in Manavgat and Antalya

Rooftop PV generates electricity on homes, businesses and industrial buildings. Site assessment, engineering, equipment selection, installation and maintenance are planned together.

Is my roof suitable?

Area, orientation, slope, shading and structural capacity are assessed together. Electricity use and connection conditions also determine system size.

What should a quote include?

A quote should specify panel and inverter models, DC capacity, mounting, electrical protection, labour and commissioning. Battery, monitoring and warranty terms should be explicit.

Cost and payback

Cost depends on equipment, roof conditions and scope. Savings depend on generation, daytime use and tariffs. The calculator gives an estimate; the final quote follows site assessment and design.
