AI Lifeguard Support System

Every second counts in the water — maximum safety for hotel and public swimming pools.

AI-powered cameras scan your pool 24/7; when risky behaviour is detected, your lifeguards receive an alert with the swimmer's location — on their smartwatch and alarm points — within seconds.

Technology aligned with ISO 20380:2017 · GDPR/KVKK-compliant local data processing

Why is it needed?

Drowning isn't loud like in the movies. It's silent.

A real drowning usually happens within 20-60 seconds, without splashing or shouting. In a crowded hotel pool, that moment is short and silent enough to escape even the most attentive lifeguard.

Worldwide, drowning is among the leading causes of death for children aged 1-4.
Lifeguard supervision is indispensable; but the human eye has physiological limits: glare, reflections, crowds and fatigue.
Technology exists to close those gaps: a tireless extra pair of eyes watching the entire pool.
How does it work?

An extra safety layer in three steps

1 · 24/7 Video Monitoring

Cameras positioned around your pool continuously watch the entire pool surface. Retrofit installation is possible without draining the pool.

2 · AI Behaviour Analysis

The system tracks each swimmer individually and analyses risk signals such as immobility, vertical sinking posture and panic movements in real time. All video processing happens on an on-site local server.

3 · Alarm Within Seconds

On detection, lifeguards get a vibrating alert on their smartwatch, a sound-and-light alarm at poolside, and the incident location on the control panel. The lifeguard knows where to jump — not where to look.
