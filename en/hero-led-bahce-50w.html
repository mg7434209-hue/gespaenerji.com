Installation

How is it connected?

By day the panel collects energy, at night the light runs on it; the sensor and remote set how it operates.

  2. 🔋Built-in storagein the luminaire
  3. 🎛️Remote control3 colours + RGB
  4. 💡Night lighting50 W
  1. 1

    Choose the spot

  3. 3
