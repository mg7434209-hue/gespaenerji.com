Solar Maintenance and Performance Monitoring

Solar maintenance uses planned checks to monitor condition and investigate generation losses. Scope depends on equipment, location and operating needs.

Maintenance scope

Checks cover panels, connections, cables, protection and inverter alerts. Cleaning follows environmental conditions and manufacturer instructions. Electrical work requires qualified personnel.

How is reduced generation investigated?

Monitoring and fault logs are assessed with weather, season, shade and equipment condition. Comparing two days from different seasons alone does not establish a fault.

Maintenance request

Provide location, capacity, inverter model and any error code. Scope and timing are agreed after review, considering warranty terms.
